About

Equinox Sports Solutions is a London-based strategic advisory shaping the future of sport through insight, structure, and communication. We work with clubs, agencies, and private investors to design strategies that elevate brands, strengthen partnerships, and turn ambition into long-term value.

Strategy × Legal Awareness × Storytelling

Our work sits at the intersection of brand strategy, legal awareness, and commercial storytelling. We translate complex goals into clear direction — from how athletes present themselves to how clubs communicate with investors and communities. A bridge between the creative and the corporate sides of sport, helping clients speak a language understood by both.

We believe that reputation is managed best before the crisis begins. While others repair the aftermath, we design systems that prevent it.
