About

Equinox Sports Solutions is a London-based strategic advisory shaping the future of sport through insight, structure, and communication.

We work with clubs, agencies, and private investors to design strategies that elevate brands, strengthen partnerships, and turn ambition into long-term value.

Strategy × Legal awareness × Storytelling — one framework.

An approach that blends legal-grade clarity with brand craft. Ambition turns into structures teams can execute, playbooks clients own, and communications that stand up to scrutiny.

Complex goals become clear direction — from athlete presentation to investor and community messaging.

Two worlds in equilibrium: the energy and emotion of competition balanced with the discipline and governance of strategy — creating lasting value.
