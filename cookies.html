1. About this Policy

This Cookies Policy explains how Equinox Sports Solutions Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on our website (eqnxsports.co.uk).

You can withdraw your consent or change your cookie preferences at any time.

2. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help us make the site work, improve user experience and understand how it’s used.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — required for core functionality (security, navigation). Always active.
  • Analytics cookies — privacy-friendly usage metrics (Cloudflare Web Analytics). Used only with your consent.
  • Functional / preference cookies — only if we enable optional features (e.g., embeds) or remember choices.

We do not use advertising or behavioural tracking cookies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies via your browser settings at any time. Disabling certain cookies may affect site performance.
